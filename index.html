// Who I am

About Me

🔬
Tasrin Aktar Research poster presentation
NMSU · 2025

I am a passionate researcher specializing in molecular biology, agricultural entomology, and computational genomics. My journey has taken me from Bangladesh to Japan and now to the United States — each chapter deepening my commitment to understanding plant–insect interactions and sustainable pest management.

Currently, I investigate how Leucaena species respond to psyllid herbivory using RNA-seq and differential gene expression analysis at the Department of Biology, New Mexico State University.

My earlier thesis at Shizuoka University explored the efficacy of entomopathogenic fungi against agriculturally significant insect pests — research at the intersection of biocontrol and applied entomology.

// Scientific Work

Research

Ongoing · Thesis
Using Differential Gene Expression Analysis to Investigate Heteropsylla cubana Herbivory Responses in the Genus Leucaena

Applying RNA-seq and DESeq2 to uncover molecular mechanisms underlying plant defense against psyllid herbivory in Leucaena. Aims to identify candidate genes for resistance breeding.

🏛 Department of Biology · NMSU, Las Cruces, NM · 2025–Present
Completed · Master's Thesis
Comparative Study of Pathogenicity and Compatibility in Entomopathogenic Fungi against Myzus persicae and Tenebrio molitor

Evaluated virulence of Metarhizium anisopliae, Beauveria bassiana, and Paecilomyces cinnamomeus isolates. Investigated mealworm extraction media effects on spore density to optimize biocontrol formulations.

🏛 Laboratory of Applied Entomology · Shizuoka University, Japan · 2022–2024
Class Project · RNA-seq
Differential Gene Expression in Leucaena leucocephala in Response to Heteropsylla cubana Herbivory Using RNA-seq

Course project employing a full RNA-seq pipeline to analyze transcriptomic responses under psyllid feeding pressure, building foundational skills for ongoing thesis work.
